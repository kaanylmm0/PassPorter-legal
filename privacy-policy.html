1. Scope and provider

This Privacy Policy describes PassPorter: Travel Wallet, an iOS application for organizing travel documents, trips and date reminders. The provider is Mehmet Kaan Yılmam, reachable at studiolabss0@gmail.com. It also explains the separate processing involved when you visit our legal website. Effective and last updated: 7 October 2026.

2. Your documents stay on your device

PassPorter stores and processes your vault contents locally on your device. We do not operate a document-storage server, upload your documents to developer servers, or automatically synchronize your vault with a cloud service. We cannot remotely view your passport scans, travel documents, notes or vault password. No PassPorter account is required. This statement concerns vault contents: purchases, website visits, support messages and actions you choose to share or export can involve other parties, as explained below.

3. Information processed locally

Depending on what you enter or import, local data may include document titles and types; holder names and document numbers; issue and expiry dates; passport, visa, identity, ticket, booking and insurance images or PDFs; destinations, travel dates and time zones; notes; document-to-trip links; reminder schedules; and language, appearance and security preferences. Imported files may themselves contain additional personal information. Only add information you are authorized to store. These contents are used for organizing, displaying, searching, editing and reminding you about your records, not advertising, profiling or AI training.

4. Security and biometric authentication

Vault records, document attachments and stored previews are encrypted at rest. The app uses Apple security facilities, including Keychain for protected key material and the system authentication framework for optional Face ID or Touch ID. PassPorter does not receive or store your biometric templates; the operating system returns an authentication result. The vault locks and conceals sensitive views when the app moves to the background. To display a document, its contents must temporarily be decrypted in memory; viewing and sharing may require temporary local files. Temporary exported files are cleaned up after use where possible, and leftover temporary files are cleaned up on a later launch. Encryption does not protect a file after you deliberately export an unencrypted copy. No device or software can be guaranteed completely secure, especially on a compromised device. Screen-capture prevention is not guaranteed.

5. Permissions and notifications

Camera access is requested to photograph a document. Photos and file pickers let you select material to import; the app does not automatically scan your entire library. A file you choose may be downloaded by a cloud-backed file provider before local import. Notification permission enables local date reminders; reminders are scheduled on your device, not through a developer push-notification server. Lock-screen reminder text is generic by default. Authentication permissions are used for optional biometric unlocking. You may deny or revoke permissions in iOS Settings; related features may then be unavailable. The app does not request contacts, microphone or precise location for its core functions.

6. Sharing, export and backups you choose

Documents do not leave the vault automatically. When you use Share, Export or Backup, you choose a recipient, app or storage location using iOS controls. This can send a document or an encrypted backup to email, messaging, Files, iCloud Drive or another service. Those services handle the copy under their own terms and policies. A document shared as an ordinary PDF or image may be unencrypted. A vault backup is encrypted using the backup password you choose; keep both the archive and password safe. We cannot revoke recipients’ copies, erase files stored elsewhere, or recover forgotten backup passwords. Importing or deleting an item in PassPorter does not delete its original copy in Photos, Files or another app.

7. Device backups and recovery

PassPorter provides no developer-managed cloud backup or password-reset service that can decrypt your vault. The supported recovery method is your encrypted exported backup and its password. iOS, computer backups or device migration tools can independently copy app-related data depending on system settings and platform behavior; this is distinct from PassPorter uploading documents to its own server. Do not rely on an operating-system backup to restore the vault, because security keys may not transfer. Loss of your device, vault password or backup password, an app deletion or a vault reset may make your contents permanently inaccessible. Keep a verified independent encrypted backup.

8. Purchases and Apple services

Optional Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes Apple Account, billing and payment information under its own policies. We do not receive your full card details. StoreKit makes transaction and entitlement information available to the app, such as product ID, transaction identifiers, purchase and expiry dates, renewal status and revocation information, so it can unlock Pro, restore purchases and apply plan limits. This does not transmit your travel documents to Apple as part of the purchase. The app does not send StoreKit transactions to a developer-operated purchase server. Apple may provide the developer with sales and subscription reports and, depending on your platform settings, diagnostics; such reports are separate from your vault contents. See Apple’s App Store privacy notice.

9. No ads, tracking SDKs or AI uploads

This version contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party behavioral analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not upload documents to an AI provider or use cloud OCR. We do not sell or rent vault contents, use them for targeted advertising or request advertising tracking permission. Any future change to these practices will require an updated policy and any permission or consent required by applicable law. Apple’s own platform services and the legal website have separate practices described here.

10. Support communications

If you contact us, we receive what you voluntarily send, such as your email address, message, app version and any attachment. Send only what is necessary; do not send passports, vault passwords, backup passwords or payment-card information. We use messages to answer requests, troubleshoot problems and meet legal obligations, not to access your vault remotely. Email and hosting providers may process these messages on our behalf or under their own policies. We ordinarily delete or anonymize support correspondence within 12 months after resolution, unless a longer period is required for a legal obligation or active dispute. You may request earlier deletion, subject to such obligations.

11. Legal website and external links

Our legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. Opening this website therefore involves network processing even though the vault is local. We have not added advertising cookies, analytics scripts, remote fonts or document-upload forms to these pages. External links lead to services governed by their own privacy policies. We do not embed your vault data in legal-page URLs. GitHub and Apple may process data in countries other than yours under their own safeguards and policies.

12. Retention and deletion

Vault records remain on your device until you remove them, reset the vault or delete the app’s local data. You can edit and delete individual records or use the app’s delete-all-data control. Resetting a forgotten-password vault destroys access rather than recovering it. iOS may retain some Keychain information separately from app files; use the in-app delete/reset function where possible before uninstalling. We do not promise forensic erasure from flash storage. Copies in external backups, recipients’ apps, original imports or operating-system backups must be managed separately. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple’s required transaction records.

13. Your choices and privacy rights

You control local contents directly: access and correct records, export documents or an encrypted backup, delete records and revoke permissions. We cannot provide a remote copy of a vault we do not hold. For personal information we do receive, for example support messages, applicable law may provide rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. Contact studiolabss0@gmail.com. Depending on the context, processing outside the local vault is based on handling your requested support/service, legitimate interests in security and resolving issues, consent where needed or legal obligations. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority. This policy does not reduce applicable KVKK, GDPR or other mandatory rights.

14. Children and other people’s documents

The app is not directed to children under 13. Users below the age of legal majority should use it with a parent or guardian’s involvement where required. An adult may store a child’s or family member’s travel documents locally only with appropriate authority and any consent required by law. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information through support. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

15. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect actual features, service providers or legal requirements. The revised date appears above; material changes will be communicated in the app or another appropriate way before taking effect where required. A future cloud or analytics feature will not be treated as already covered by a promise of local-only storage. Privacy questions: studiolabss0@gmail.com. Provider: Mehmet Kaan Yılmam.
